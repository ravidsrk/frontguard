# T-23 — scratch wrangler rollback — 2026-10-19

Target was a new Worker, `frontguard-rollback-drill`, not `frontguard-web`.
Wrangler 4.151.1. Config lived in `/tmp/fg-rollback-drill` (`workers_dev: true`).
`frontguard-web` was not rolled back. After the drill, the scratch Worker was
deleted and its workers.dev URL returned HTTP 404. The production worker still

version) and deployed it to 100% of traffic.
